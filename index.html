What Is DA News?

DA News, short for Definitely Accurate News, is a student-operated broadcasting network dedicated to keeping University of South Carolina students informed and entertained. We deliver campus news, student interviews, sports updates, and breaking stories through a modern digital platform. Our mission is to make campus information accessible, engaging, and trustworthy.

Powered by Technology

Behind every DA News broadcast is a digital information system. Our IT team manages website development, database organization, cybersecurity, and an AI-assisted content verification process. Technology helps us deliver accurate information while protecting our broadcasting platform.
